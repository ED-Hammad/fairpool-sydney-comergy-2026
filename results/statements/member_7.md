## How your share was worked out

Every possible group of households was costed as if it ran its own pool (511 groups). Your fair share is the average amount the saving grew whenever you joined a group, taken over every order in which the community could have formed. That average is 1744 AUD, which is 37.4 % of the community saving (this is the Shapley value).

Ten percent of the community saving is set aside for lower-income members; your gain is 90 % of your Shapley share.

For comparison, a rule that gives everyone the same percentage saving would give you 1276 AUD.
