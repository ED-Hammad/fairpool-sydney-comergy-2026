## How your share was worked out

Every possible group of households was costed as if it ran its own pool (511 groups). Your fair share is the average amount the saving grew whenever you joined a group, taken over every order in which the community could have formed. That average is 694 AUD, which is 14.9 % of the community saving (this is the Shapley value).

Because your household income is below the community threshold, you also receive 233 AUD from the solidarity share (10 % of the community saving set aside for lower-income members). Your total gain is 90 % of your Shapley share plus this amount.

For comparison, a rule that gives everyone the same percentage saving would give you 840 AUD.
